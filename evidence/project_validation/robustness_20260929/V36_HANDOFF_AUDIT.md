# v36 retained-contact handoff audit (read-only)

Source: frozen `robust_v36_20260929/runs/completion_full400_retry3` on the server, selected final attempts for all 400 scenes. The raw selected-attempt classification and representative terminal HDF5 samples are in `v36_handoff_audit.json` and `v36_handoff_trace_audit.json`. This audit does not rerun physics or modify an episode.

`contact_handoff_aborted` is a wrapper applied when an operation fails while `retained_contact` is set. The 32 selected failures comprise:

| Underlying error | Scenes | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `ik_unreachable` | 12 | Push path planning rejected before a free-open candidate; not a contact observation. TCP position errors were about 12.8–17.0 mm in the recorded rejected queries. |
| `retained_contact_lost` | 8 | Actual target-tray table contacts fell to zero for 0.35 s despite 10–15 N at the retained hand. All 8 had a free-open candidate. |
| `primitive_postcondition_failed` | 9 | Eight had a free-open candidate; the released side still exerted about 13–19 N near the end of push and only 1–2 table contacts remained. One (`0394`) had no free-open candidate and retained support alone failed the settle predicate. |
| `measured_joint_overspeed` | 1 | Actual left joint 5 speed ratio 1.056 exceeded the configured 1.05 completion cap. |
| `primitive_release_not_verified` | 1 | Actual place-release force remained nonzero (11.0 and 3.95 N); independent of push free-open. |
| `joining_arm_path_unavailable` | 1 | All seven joining-arm preflight candidates rejected by IK before push. |

The free-open option **did activate in 17 of these 32 selected failure scenes**, emitting 18 candidates; one scene emitted two. Its reference was physically executed, not merely logged: terminal HDF5 `action` rows show the released jaw progressing to the 0.10 m open command in the postcondition group. It is therefore wrong to diagnose the option as disabled. The actual freed-hand force fell to zero and then rose again in seven of the eight support-loss traces; the tray center subsequently rose while table contacts disappeared. In `0110`, for example, the tray moved from y = −0.463 m to −0.555 m while the free right TCP moved from y = −0.668 m to −0.705 m and rose from z = 0.893 m to 0.971 m. Its actual contact force rose from 0 to 14.1 N, and tray center rose to z = 0.822 m. Opening the fingers while moving the free wrist upward concurrently with the retained push did not prevent the whole hand from recontacting the oncoming tray. The free-side finger geometry, not just TCP distance, matters. This is an inference from the recorded trajectory; the trace does not directly label the exact colliding free-hand geom.

The current `push()` path already has a `withdraw_free` mode with a free-arm-first intermediate knot inside one C2 primitive and `plan_withdraw_path()` bounded duration alternatives. This mode activates only if an earlier lift had `primitive_lift_xy_shift_m > 0.005`; the observed free-open candidates are the other branch, whose free arm rises concurrently with the push. A minimal next experiment should create a free-first knot for retained pushes in that branch, place the free arm outside the **swept, yaw-aware tray footprint and rim** before the retained arm advances, and keep the entire reference within the same primitive budget. A fixed 6 cm retreat along base x is insufficient for pushes along y; in `0110`, the oncoming tray edge approaches the free right hand along negative y. Try bounded clearance offsets along the actual push direction, plus upward motion, then run the existing full C2 path, collision, wrist and dynamics checks. Add a planning-only check of the moving tray footprint against the free hand and fingers at trajectory samples. The actual episode must still pass the existing live retained-force, table-support and released-hand-force guards. An analytic sweep is only a candidate screen, not proof that the dynamics will remain supported.

Priority: this path could address up to 16 of the 32 selected wrapped failures with physical recontact/support mechanisms; it cannot address the 12 preplanning IK cases or the separate release/overspeed cases. Verify on fresh small scene groups before another full collection and report all new physical failures and successes. Do not broaden `retained_contact_lost` or contact-force acceptance to hide the observed lift.
